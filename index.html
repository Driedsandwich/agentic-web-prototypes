Agentic web prototypes

Clickable prototypes for five open agentic web tools. Each one runs on sample data only: nothing is sent, no AI model runs, and nothing costs money.

  • Edition SwitchCheck each photo's terms when a flyer moves to the web, and agree on a replacement offered by the rights holder's network.
  • Benefit CommonsEstimate likely public benefits in your browser from rule copies published on separate sites. Your values stay here.
  • ModelproofSee where your agent network breaks across models, harden its instructions and publish a compatibility declaration.
  • PliantA form that reshapes itself with reversible changes when you pause, and tells you why.
  • DocmeshOne question answered by documentation networks on separate sites, with a source and license for every sentence.
